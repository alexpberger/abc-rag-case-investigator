This statement was taken from Jordan Hale, the named insured on fictional policy NL-EXAMPLE-2041, regarding the dwelling at 18 Paperbirch Court, Example Harbour. It records what the customer said. It is not a finding about the pipe, and it is not a request that the claim be paid. Northline Example Mutual took the statement on 4 March 2026.

# What the customer reported

Jordan Hale reported that a supply pipe under the kitchen sink began to leak and that the leak was sudden. The customer said the cabinet floor was dry in the morning and was wet by early afternoon, with water standing at the toe kick. The customer described the event as a sudden pipe leak and said the water appeared without warning.

The customer shut off the angle stop under the sink and called Example Harbour Plumbing the same day. The customer said the wet materials were paper cabinet flooring, the lower face of the sink cabinet, and a strip of hardwood just outside the cabinet. No other rooms were said to have standing water. The customer asked that the damaged cabinet and the nearby floor be included in the claim.

# When the water was discovered

The customer places the discovery on 3 March 2026, during the early afternoon. The customer does not give a clock time. The customer was at home, opened the cabinet to retrieve a cleaning bottle, and saw water on the cabinet floor. The customer had not opened that cabinet earlier on 3 March and does not describe any check of the cabinet on 2 March.

The customer cannot say how long water had been leaving the pipe before it was seen. The statement uses the phrase "it must have just started" and does not tie that phrase to a sound, a pressure change, or a moment the customer heard or saw the leak begin. No one else in the household gave a statement. The discovery time is an afternoon window on 3 March 2026, not an observed start of the escape.

# Rooms affected

The reported damage is in the kitchen. The customer identified the sink cabinet, the toe kick beneath it, and approximately one square metre of hardwood immediately in front of the cabinet. The customer said the hardwood felt damp at the edges and dry further into the room. Walls, the ceiling below, and the dishwasher were described as unaffected.

The customer aired the cabinet and placed a household fan at the toe kick. No restoration contractor has attended. The customer has not removed the cabinet floor. The wet materials were still in place when the statement was taken, except for towels the customer used to pick up standing water. Those towels were not kept.

# Earlier problems

The customer denied earlier problems with this pipe. The customer said there had been no prior leak under the sink, no stain inside the cabinet, and no previous repair to the supply lines. The customer has lived in the dwelling for six years and said the kitchen plumbing was already installed at purchase. The customer does not know the age of the pipe and does not have the seller's disclosure to hand.

The denial of earlier problems is the customer's account. The statement contains no invoices, no earlier photographs, and no names of a previous plumber. The customer said any paperwork from the purchase is in a storage box that has not been searched. The customer agreed to look and did not, in this statement, produce a document.

# Materials not in the file

The customer referred to two photographs taken on a phone on 3 March 2026, one of the wet cabinet floor and one of the pipe. The photographs were not attached to the statement and had not been received by Northline Example Mutual when this note was written. No moisture reading taken by the customer is in the file, because the customer did not take one.

The statement does not include a maintenance record, a pipe invoice, or a written age for the supply line. It includes the customer's description of a sudden leak, the discovery window on 3 March 2026, the rooms and materials said to be wet, and the denial of earlier trouble. Those are statements. The photographs and the maintenance papers the customer mentioned are not in this document.
