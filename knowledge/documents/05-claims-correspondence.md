This note collects the claim correspondence on fictional file NL-EXAMPLE-2041, Jordan Hale, 18 Paperbirch Court, Example Harbour. The handler is S. Patel at Northline Example Mutual. The messages below are the request for records and the reply that came back. No coverage position is stated in either message. The file remains open.

# Request for records

On 7 March 2026, S. Patel wrote to Jordan Hale. The message acknowledged the claim statement of 4 March 2026 and the plumbing inspection of 6 March 2026. It said both documents were in the file and that the file was not ready for a decision. The handler asked for four items: the maintenance history for the kitchen supply plumbing, any record of the age of the pipe, the two photographs the statement said were taken on 3 March 2026, and a short note of whether any dampness or stain had been noticed under the sink before that date.

The message explained that the policy question includes both sudden and accidental discharge and the exclusions for gradual deterioration and for seepage or leakage over time, and that the adjuster cannot apply those sections without the requested records. The handler did not predict an outcome. The handler set a reply date of 21 March 2026 and offered to accept photographs by the same email thread. The message also asked the customer not to discard the affected supply line if it is later replaced, so it can be looked at if needed.

# Customer reply

Jordan Hale replied on 11 March 2026. The reply says the customer looked in the household papers and did not find a plumbing invoice, a maintenance log, or a document that states the age of the pipe. The customer repeated that there had been no earlier leak and that the cabinet had not shown a stain before 3 March 2026. The reply does not attach the photographs requested on 7 March. It says the phone photos "will be sent if they can be found," and it does not include an image.

The reply does not contain a prior-leak history beyond the repeated denial. It names no earlier plumber, no earlier date, and no repair. It does not contradict the inspection note point by point, and it does not discuss the stain the plumber described. The handler's four requests are therefore only partly addressed: the customer reports that the maintenance history and the pipe age were not found, restates the denial of earlier trouble, and does not provide the photographs.

# No decision recorded

No further message from the handler is in the file after 11 March 2026. No adjuster has written a payment, a denial, or a reservation of rights. The correspondence does not close the claim. S. Patel's request remains open on the items the reply did not supply, in particular the photographs and any record that would show maintenance or pipe age rather than their absence.

The file, as correspondence, shows a request, an incomplete reply, and no decision. Anyone reading only these messages can see that Northline Example Mutual asked for the maintenance history and the prior leak history in the form of documents and a specific note, and that the customer answered from memory and without the papers or the photographs. The correspondence stops there. It does not say what the adjuster will do when the reply date passes, and it does not treat the incomplete reply as the end of the investigation.
