This note collects the correspondence on fictional file LN-EXAMPLE-2041, Jens Hansen, Birkevej 18, 2. tv., Eksempelhavn. The case worker is S. Patel at Berger Bank. The messages below are the request for records and the reply that came back. No lending position is stated in either message. The file remains open.

# Request for records

On 7 October 2026, S. Patel wrote to Jens Hansen. The message acknowledged the application of 2 October 2026, the income papers received on 3 October 2026, and the internal comparison of 6 October 2026. It said those documents were in the file and that the file was not ready for a decision. The case worker asked for four items: an employer letter covering employment before the payslip start date of 3 February 2026, a written explanation of whether the August overtime is regular, the source of the DKK 18,500 credit on 18 September 2026, and the year-end income document the application said would follow.

The message explained that the policy question includes verified income, employment tenure, and documented obligations, and that a lender cannot apply those sections while the requested records are missing. The case worker did not predict an outcome. The case worker set a reply date of 21 October 2026 and offered to accept the documents on the same email thread.

# Customer reply

Jens Hansen replied on 11 October 2026. The reply says the employer letter will be sent if human resources provides it. The reply says the overtime is regular. It does not attach a further payslip, a roster, or any other record of overtime. It does not mention the DKK 18,500 credit. It does not attach the year-end income document.

The reply does not name an employer before Nordlinje A/S, and it does not give a date before 3 February 2026. It does not discuss the bureau balance. The case worker's four requests are therefore only partly addressed: the applicant defers the employer letter, restates that overtime is regular, and does not provide the source of the credit or the year-end document.

# No decision recorded

No further message from the case worker is in the file after 11 October 2026. No lender has written an approval, a decline, or a recommended outcome. The correspondence does not close the application. S. Patel's request remains open on the items the reply did not supply, in particular the employer letter, the source of the credit, and the year-end document.

The file, as correspondence, shows a request, an incomplete reply, and no decision. Anyone reading only these messages can see that Berger Bank asked for documents and a specific explanation, and that the applicant answered in part and without those papers. The correspondence stops there. It does not say what the lender will do when the reply date passes, and it does not treat the incomplete reply as the end of the investigation.
