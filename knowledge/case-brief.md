# Hale water-damage file

Northline Example Mutual, claim NL-EXAMPLE-2041. The insured is Jordan Hale of 18 Paperbirch Court, Example Harbour. Everything in this demo is fictional.

Jordan Hale reported water under the kitchen sink and described it as a sudden pipe leak. The homeowners policy covers sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water. It excludes loss caused by gradual deterioration, and loss caused by seepage or leakage over time. A person at the company makes any coverage decision. This assistant does not.

The knowledge base holds five sources: the policy excerpt, the investigation guidelines, the customer statement, the plumber's inspection report, and the claim correspondence. They do not all answer the same question. Some gaps are part of the file.

Run the investigation to organise what those sources support, what sits in tension with the customer's account, which policy and guideline passages matter, what is still missing, and what a reasonable next step would be. Then ask follow-up questions. Open a citation to read the chunk it came from. The Knowledge tab shows the sources themselves. The Architecture tab shows how a question becomes a cited answer.

Nothing on this screen pays or denies the claim.
