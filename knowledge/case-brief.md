# Hansen personal-loan file

- **Customer:** Jens Hansen
- **Address:** 18 Paperbirch Court, Example Harbour
- **Application:** LN-EXAMPLE-2041
- **Lender:** Berger Bank
- **Applied:** 2 October 2026
- **Loan requested:** €15,000 over 48 months
- **Purpose:** Kitchen repairs
- **Stated employment:** Facilities coordinator at Northline Facilities since March 2022
- **Stated income:** €4,200 gross per month
- **Stated obligations:** Rent of €1,100; one credit card, paid in full each month; no other loans

Jens Hansen applied for a personal loan and described long continuous employment, one monthly income figure, and modest obligations. The credit policy takes income, employment tenure, and obligations from documents in the file. Stated income and irregular overtime are not enough on their own. A person at the bank makes any lending decision. This assistant does not.

The knowledge base holds five sources: the credit policy, the loan application, the income documents, the credit risk note, and the correspondence. They do not all answer the same question. Some gaps are part of the file.

Run the investigation to organise what those sources support, which concerns or inconsistencies sit beside the application, which policy passages matter, what is still missing, and what a reasonable next step would be. Then ask follow-up questions. Open a citation to read the chunk it came from. The Knowledge tab shows the sources themselves. The Architecture tab shows how a question becomes a cited answer.
